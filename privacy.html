Filingo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. Data Collection and Use

Filingo is committed to protecting your privacy. Our app does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.

  • We do not require account creation or personal information.
  • We do not track your usage behavior.
  • We do not use any analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

2. App Permissions

Filingo requests only the following necessary permissions:

  • Network access: to retrieve publicly available SEC filing data from the EDGAR system.

We do not request access to your contacts, photos, location, or other sensitive data.

3. Data Sources

Filingo uses only data publicly provided by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) via the EDGAR system:

  • Company information and stock ticker mappings.
  • Filing history and metadata.
  • Financial data (XBRL format).
  • Complete filing documents (HTML, XML, PDF, etc.).

All data is obtained directly from SEC official servers. We do not modify, cache (beyond temporary network caching), or redistribute any of this data.

4. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store user data, there is no user data at risk of exposure. Temporary caches used for performance are cleared when the app restarts.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the revision date will be updated. Please review this page periodically for any changes.
