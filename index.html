Filingo

An iOS app focused on US stocks: manage your watchlist, view company details and SEC filings.

Features

📊 Watchlist Management

Search and manage your US stock watchlist, with each row showing company name, exchange, and the most recent filing type.

📈 Stock Details

Company overview + fundamental trends (revenue / net income / EPS from SEC XBRL data) + latest 5 filings.

📋 Filings Browser

Three-level browsing: Company → Form Type (with counts) → Specific filing.

📄 Filing Reader

Embedded WebView loads raw documents directly from sec.gov.

Compliance

Filingo only displays data publicly released by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC); it shows no market data.

Data Sources and Legal Basis

  • Only data source: SEC EDGAR
  • Stock ticker → CIK: https://www.sec.gov/files/company_tickers_exchange.json
  • Filing history: https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK##########.json
  • Financial data (XBRL): https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK##########.json
  • Filing documents: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/{cik}/{accession}/{document}

Copyright Notice

Under 17 U.S.C. §105 and 15 U.S.C. §78ll, SEC filings are U.S. government works and are not subject to copyright. Federal law permits EDGAR information to be used, resold, or redistributed without restriction and without additional fees or royalties.

Important note: Filings may contain third-party content (e.g., licensed material, images, glossaries in exhibits). This app presents SEC documents exactly as filed—no extraction, no reformatting, no relicensing of such content.
